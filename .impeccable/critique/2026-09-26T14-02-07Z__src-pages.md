## Design Specificity
- Admin: partly specific (plum sidebar, Rs., bridal suite, local wallets, EID20). Dashboard structure is stock SaaS (6 equal KPI cards + chart + 2 lists). Schedule is the most product-specific screen.
- Public site: template luxury formula (tracked-caps eyebrow → serif headline with one italic pink word → numbered cards → stats strip → testimonials → "Ready to look iconic?"). Hero photo washed out and shows another salon's "…EAUTY SALON" sign. Mehndi section is the one with real character.
- Portal: different visual language from admin (square corners, tracked caps).
- Detector (CLI): 1 finding — side-tab border-l-4 on schedule blocks (calendar convention, low priority). Browser overlay: 16–64 per page; real: low-contrast (white on #ee2b93 3.9:1; pink on cream 3.3:1), undersized 9–10px labels in all three layouts, kicker-above-heading ×9 on landing, glow shadows + pulsing dot in admin chrome, em-dash overuse (24) on landing.
